78h gross capacity − 12h platform operation = 66h net capacity. Redeploying 50% yields 33h. Recovering 480h of setup requires 15 comparable packs, rounded up.

Adoption prerequisites are unverified. All hours are conditional assumptions. Assisted work and review are separate, non-overlapping buckets. The baseline includes its ordinary review and rework. Setup includes integration, coaching and supporting staff time. Additional tool, cloud and vendor charges are excluded. This is capacity arithmetic, not financial ROI or an isolated AI productivity estimate. The release pack spans an assumed 10 business days; no shorter release cycle is claimed.
