# Governance

Regulatory summaries on these pages are research notes verified against the primary source on the date stated. They are not legal advice; a bank's second line and legal counsel should confirm how each instrument applies to a specific pilot.

The governance context is a strength for a conservative AI-QE proposal, not a constraint on it. The control boundaries that a low-risk pilot adopts anyway (human approval before merge, scoped non-human identities, prompt and model logging, model-inventory registration, approved platforms only) are the same practices the Canadian prudential regulator published in 2026.
