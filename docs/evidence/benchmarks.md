## Controlled and independent studies

| Source and date | Sample and method | Metric and denominator | Measured or self-reported; sponsor | Supports | Headline finding | Caveats |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| [METR, "Measuring the Impact of Early-2025 AI on Experienced Open-Source Developer Productivity"](https://metr.org/blog/2025-07-10-early-2025-ai-experienced-os-dev-study/), 10 Jul 2025 ([arXiv 2507.09089](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.09089)) | 16 experienced maintainers, 246 real issues, randomized AI-allowed vs not; Cursor Pro with Claude 3.5/3.7 Sonnet | Time per issue | Measured (screen recording); independent non-profit | Task efficiency (negative) | AI-allowed issues took 19% longer (CI +2% to +39%); developers expected 24% faster and afterwards believed 20% faster | Elite developers on repositories they know well; small sample; authors note possible learning effects after several hundred hours |
| [METR, "We are changing our developer productivity experiment design"](https://metr.org/blog/2026-02-24-uplift-update/), 24 Feb 2026 | 57 developers, 800+ tasks, 143 repositories, late-2025 tools | Time per task | Measured; independent | Null to negative, unreliable | -18% (CI -38% to +9%) original cohort; -4% (CI -15% to +9%) new cohort | 30-50% of developers withheld tasks; design abandoned for selection bias; no clean 2026 RCT exists |
| [METR, self-reported impact survey](https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-11-ai-usage-survey/), 11 May 2026 | 349 technical workers, survey | Self-estimated speed-up | Self-reported; independent | Perception only | Median self-reported uplift about 3x | Authors: their own RCT participants over-estimated by 40 points |
| [Peng, Kalliamvakou, Cihon, Demirer, "The Impact of AI on Developer Productivity: Evidence from GitHub Copilot"](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.06590), 13 Feb 2023 | 95 freelancers, one JavaScript HTTP-server task, randomized | Task completion time | Measured; vendor-affiliated (Microsoft Research, GitHub, MIT) | Task efficiency | 55.8% faster (71.2 vs 160.9 minutes; CI 21% to 89%) | Single synthetic task; code quality not assessed |
| [Cui et al., "The Effects of Generative AI on High-Skilled Work: Evidence from Three Field Experiments"](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=4945566), SSRN Sep 2024; [Management Science](https://pubsonline.informs.org/doi/10.1287/mnsc.2025.00535) Feb 2026 | 4,867 developers at three firms, randomized Copilot access | Completed tasks (pull requests) per developer | Measured telemetry; Microsoft-affiliated | Output volume | +26.1% completed tasks (standard error 10.3); larger gains for less experienced developers | Wide interval; no quality, throughput or cost measure |
| [GitHub and Accenture enterprise study](https://github.blog/news-insights/research/research-quantifying-github-copilots-impact-in-the-enterprise-with-accenture/), 13 May 2024 | Randomized, sample size not disclosed | PRs, merge rate, build success | Telemetry plus survey; vendor | Output volume | +8.7% PRs, +15% merge rate, +84% successful builds | No confidence intervals or n published |
| [GitHub code-quality RCT](https://github.blog/news-insights/research/does-github-copilot-improve-code-quality-heres-what-the-data-says/), 18 Nov 2024 | 202 developers, blinded review by 25 developers | Unit-test pass rate, quality scores | Measured; vendor | Quality (small effect) | 53% more likely to pass all unit tests; readability, reliability, maintainability +2 to +4% | Artificial task |
| [McKinsey, "Unleashing developer productivity with generative AI"](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/mckinsey-digital/our-insights/unleashing-developer-productivity-with-generative-ai), 27 Jun 2023 | About 40 in-house developers, lab tasks | Task time | Measured (lab); consultancy | Task efficiency | Documentation in about half the time; code generation about half; refactoring about two thirds; under 10% saving on complex tasks; junior developers 7-10% slower | Tiny in-house sample; lab conditions |

| [DORA / Google Cloud, Accelerate State of DevOps 2024](https://dora.dev/research/2024/dora-report/), 22 Oct 2024 ([announcement](https://cloud.google.com/blog/products/devops-sre/announcing-the-2024-dora-report)) | About 3,000 survey respondents; regression on AI adoption | Change per 25% increase in AI adoption | Self-reported; Google Cloud with sponsors | Individual perception up; delivery down | Throughput -1.5%, stability -7.2%, documentation quality +7.5%, code quality +3.4%, review speed +3.1%; 39% little or no trust in AI code | Cross-sectional association, not causal |

| [DORA, ROI of AI-assisted Software Development](https://dora.dev/ai/roi/report/), May 2026 ([coverage](https://www.infoq.com/news/2026/05/dora-roi-ai-assisted-dev-report/)) | Framework with an illustrative 500-engineer organization | Modelled ROI | Modelled; Google Cloud; full PDF gated | Cost-benefit model only | Illustrative 39% first-year ROI, about 8-month payback, J-curve with an initial dip; task gains 35-40% greenfield vs 10% or less on complex legacy code | Authors call it a high-uncertainty estimate |

| [Faros AI, "AI Engineering Report 2026: The Acceleration Whiplash"](https://www.faros.ai/blog/ai-acceleration-whiplash-takeaways), 12 Apr 2026 | 22,000 developers, 4,000+ teams; lowest- vs highest-adoption periods | Pre/post deltas | Measured; vendor | Throughput up, quality down | +33.7% throughput, +16.2% merge rate, +54% bugs per developer, +242.7% incidents-to-PR ratio, +441.5% review time, +31.3% PRs merged without review | Not controlled; may not generalize |
| [Uplevel, Copilot impact study](https://uplevelteam.com/blog/ai-for-developer-productivity) (2024; page updated 2026) | About 800 developers, telemetry | Bugs, cycle time | Measured; analytics vendor | Contradictory evidence | No significant productivity gain; 41% higher bug-introduction rate | Single customer base |
| [Atlassian, State of Developer Experience 2025](https://www.atlassian.com/teams/software-development/state-of-developer-experience-2025), 9 Jul 2025 | 3,500 developers and managers, six countries | Hours per week | Self-reported; vendor | Perceived time saved | 68% save 10+ hours per week with AI; 50% lose 10+ hours to friction; 16% of time spent coding | Perception |
| [Stack Overflow Developer Survey 2025](https://survey.stackoverflow.co/2025/ai), Jul 2025 | 49,000+ respondents, 177 countries | Share of developers | Self-reported | Adoption and trust | 84% use or plan to use AI; 46% distrust accuracy vs 33% trust; 66% cite "almost right" code; 45% say debugging AI code takes longer; 17.9% use AI mostly for testing | 2026 survey not yet public at verification date |
| [BCG, AI at Work 2026](https://www.bcg.com/press/3june2026-ai-reshaping-jobs-faster-than-companies-reshaping-work), 3 Jun 2026 | 11,749 workers, 14 markets | Share of workers | Self-reported; consultancy | Time saved not redirected | 42% save a workday or more weekly; 66% get no guidance on what to do with saved time | Cross-industry |
| [MIT NANDA, "The GenAI Divide: State of AI in Business 2025"](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/mit-report-95-generative-ai-105412686.html), Jul 2025 | 52 interviews, 153 survey responses, 300 deployments | Share of pilots with P&L impact | Self-reported; academic preliminary | Enterprise pilot to P&L gap | "95% of organizations are getting zero return"; 5% of integrated pilots extract value | Authors: "directionally accurate"; not software-specific |
