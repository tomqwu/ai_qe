08 / Capability roadmap

Expansion follows repeatable evidence

Foundations

Reliable inputs

Classified context, repeatable tests, baseline and owners.

Bounded assistance

Useful workflows

Test drafts and failure diagnosis with independent checks.

Connected assurance

Shared evidence

Versioned evaluations, telemetry and tested fallback.

Broader authority

Controlled agents

More complex work with action-level evidence and clear limits.

Proposed capability stages. Timing depends on the starting system and consequences of failure.

09 / Technology strategy

The quality system outlasts a model

Task contracts

Define correct outcomes and important failures.

Evaluation assets

Maintain representative cases and trusted labels.

Platform controls

Reuse identity, policy and evidence services.

Portable evidence

Keep comparisons and history when tools change.

Buy and integrate against a common evaluation brief. Product features do not establish local effectiveness.

10 / Leadership agenda

The strategic choices ahead

Ambition

Quality as a shared capability

Choose the delivery constraints and AI risks the organization needs to address.

Investment

Foundations and people

Develop evaluation skills, platform integration and accountable ownership.

Expansion

Value with evidence

Grow the capabilities that improve outcomes within their control boundary.

Vision: AI contributes more work while the organization strengthens its ability to judge and govern that work.
