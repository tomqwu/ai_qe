Executive perspective / Strategic vision

A stronger quality system.
More capacity for change.

AI-assisted quality engineering & application security

Embed assistance where teams work. Keep accountability where it belongs.A strategic direction, grounded in research.
Bank-specific outcomes remain to be proven.

02 / The strategic shift

Build a quality capability
that improves with use.

The ambition: consistent assistance across the delivery lifecycle, with stronger feedback and clear ownership.

01 / Assist the work

Reduce repetitive effort

Help teams classify failures, draft tests and prepare remediation.

02 / Strengthen the system

Keep quality visible

Pair assistance with review discipline, deterministic checks and traceable decisions.

03 / Learn across teams

Reuse what proves useful

Share evaluated workflows and patterns after results repeat in delivery.

A proposed direction. External task gains do not establish enterprise productivity or savings.
{% include slide-footer.html n="02" source="/docs/evidence/reading-the-evidence/" label="Evidence and its limits" %}

03 / Where to focus

Start with work that is frequent,
bounded and measurable.

Quality engineering

Understand test failures

Classify failed tests and prepare the evidence a team needs to act.

Quality engineering

Strengthen test coverage

Draft API and component tests from specifications and existing tests.

Optional / AppSec

Prioritize security work

Support finding triage, owner routing and draft fixes with human review.

Begin on approved platforms in one or two applications. Fix foundational engineering gaps where they are the real constraint.

05 / The trust model

Trust is part of the architecture.

People remain accountable

Humans review and approve changes. Decision ownership stays explicit.

Checks remain deterministic

Existing test, security and change controls govern release.

Access stays bounded

Approved models, scoped identities and non-production data.

Evidence stays traceable

Record prompts, model versions, artifacts, approvals and operating cost.

{% include slide-footer.html n="05" source="/docs/governance/control-mapping/" label="Pilot control mapping" %}

06 / The path to scale

Scale capability as
the evidence becomes stronger.

Align + baseline

Understand the work

Agree the scope, establish the baseline and name the value owner.

Capped pilot

Prove usefulness

Measure net effort, adoption, cost and quality in selected workflows.

Production validation

Prove repeatability

Expand to additional teams and observe at least two releases.

Incremental scale

Invest from proof

Expand successful capabilities with a business case for each.

Shared platform investment follows validated need and economic value.
