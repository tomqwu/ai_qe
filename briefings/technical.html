08 / Measurement architecture

A joined record of work and outcomes

Task

ID, complexity, user cohort and baseline effort.

Run

Context, model, prompts, tools, runtime and cost.

Review

Checks, decision, correction and active human effort.

Outcome

Rework, defects, delivery time and realized benefit.

Version and join these records under the logging standard. Minimize sensitive payload retention.

10 / Proposed pilot promotion

Value within quality and control floors

≥15%

Net effort saving

On at least one selected use case, after review and control work.

≥50%

Eligible-task adoption

Actual workflow use, with acceptance and abandonment recorded.

Within cap

Operating cost

Agreed ceiling and a Finance-confirmed capture mechanism.

All floors must hold

Quality, security and control requirements remain satisfied. A breach pauses the pilot and triggers review.

Proposed criteria from the existing pilot plan, not achieved results. The plan specifies stop signals and the 10–15% review band.
